# FieldBuilt Positioning — The Holy Trio (Time · Money · Revenue), flooring edition

## The position
You have the crews, not the measures. FieldBuilt runs the ads that bring homeowners in, answers
every one of them in seconds, qualifies them, books them as in-home measures with every decision
maker home, and chases every quote until it signs or says no. Installed by Jonathan, plugged into
the CRM or calendar you already use.

The offer, exact wording: **Guaranteed 20 in-home measures in 60 days, and every quote chased
until it signs or says no.** No price and no guarantee failure terms on anything public.

The trio is one sentence with three consequences: crews stay on installs because the measures
keep coming (**revenue**), nobody in the shop spends the evening calling leads back or chasing
quotes (**time**), and no more paying for shared leads sold to four other companies or driving
45 minutes to a measure that was never going to sign (**money**). Revenue leads; the other two
follow as proof.

## The mechanism (always in this order)
1. We run the ads: Meta ads for homeowners in your service area. You pay the ad spend straight
   to Meta, separate from our fee.
2. Every lead answered in seconds, 24/7, by text and phone.
3. Qualified before it touches the calendar: project type (LVP, hardwood, carpet, tile,
   laminate), rough size or rooms, timeline, ZIP in your area, every decision maker home.
4. Booked as an in-home measure on real estimator and crew availability, with confirmation,
   a day-before reminder and an easy reschedule path.
5. Every quote chased after the measure, by text, call and email, until it signs or says no.

## Discipline
Never "revolutionary," "game-changing," or "AI-powered" as a benefit. Never "software",
"platform", "SaaS", "tool", "app", "solution", "streamline", "leverage", "seamless", "unlock".
Every claim is a number, a time of day, or something the reader can check. Confidence lives in
specificity. Claim standard: "in seconds" for speed claims. Never invent flooring numbers,
testimonials or client names. Never claim one company per area until Jonathan confirms it.
Example conversations are always labelled as examples.
