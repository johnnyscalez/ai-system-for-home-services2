## Changed

### ICP
Flooring contractors in the USA **with their own installation crews**: shop-at-home flooring
companies, flooring stores/showrooms that install, and installer-led flooring contractors.
The owner has crews who can install more than the calendar currently feeds them.
No revenue threshold is set yet; do not invent one.

Not a fit: one-person installers with no crew, installers who only sub for stores, anyone
outside the USA.

### The offer (exact wording)
**Guaranteed 20 in-home measures in 60 days, and every quote chased until it signs or says no.**

- /book headline variant, set by Jonathan 2026-10-20: "Get 20+ Shown In-Home Measures in 60 Days, and Every Quote Chased Until It Books. Guaranteed" with the sub "...qualified flooring leads contacted, answered and booked within 2 seconds, to guarantee you 20+ shown in-home measures on autopilot." The homepage keeps the original wording above.
- Never state what happens if the 20 aren't hit (no refund / "you don't pay" / "we work free"
  wording). The guarantee terms are not public yet.
- Never show or quote price on any public page or ad. Price is discussed on the call only.
  Internal reference: $5,997 install + $799/month (CLAUDE.md), unconfirmed for flooring.

### How it works (the mechanism, in this order)
1. **We run the ads.** FieldBuilt builds and runs Meta ads for homeowners in the contractor's
   service area. The contractor pays the ad spend directly to Meta; that is separate from our fee.
2. **Every lead answered in seconds**, 24/7, by text and phone. Where a number is used, it is "1 second" (Jonathan, 2026-10-28; was "2 seconds" on /book).
   Speed stats allowed in copy (sources checked): Service Direct 2022 survey of 559 homeowners (85% contact 3 or fewer
   contractors, 60% decide within 72 hours, almost 40% rarely/never hire one who missed their first call); MIT/InsideSales
   2007 (21x better odds of qualifying at 5 min vs 30 min). Never use "78% hire the first to respond": no source.
3. **Qualified before it reaches the calendar**: project type (LVP, hardwood, carpet, tile,
   laminate), rough size/rooms, timeline, ZIP in the service area, and whether every decision
   maker will be home for the measure.
4. **Booked as an in-home measure** on real crew/estimator availability, with confirmation,
   day-before reminder and a reschedule path so measures don't no-show.
5. **Every quote chased** after the measure, by text, call and email, until the homeowner signs
   or says no. Nothing dies in a "let me think about it" pile.

FieldBuilt is now ads + AI booking + quote follow-up. Retire the old line "Not a lead gen agency"
and "the phone already rings" fit criteria.

### Language
- Say "in-home measure" (or "measure"), "quote", "install", "crews", "shop-at-home",
  "homeowner". Not "truck roll", "technician", "dispatch", "service call", "job ticket".
- The AI never quotes the flooring price; the quote comes from the contractor after the measure.
  It always discloses it is an AI.

### Pains to name (from the research)
- Crews ready, calendar thin. "You have the crews, not the measures."
- Shared leads (Angi, HomeAdvisor) sold to four other companies; half never pick up.
- Speed wins: homeowners contact several flooring companies at once and book whoever answers first.
- Wasted measure drives: 45 minutes out, then "my husband needs to be here before we decide."
- Quotes that go quiet: the measure happens, the quote goes out, nobody follows up.
- Winter slowdown Nov to Feb after the early-fall peak.
