The problem

The crews are ready. The calendar is thin.

Most flooring owners with their own crews can install more than the calendar feeds them. The leak isn't install quality. It's the front half of the business: getting homeowners in, getting to them first, getting the right people home for the measure, and following the quote until it closes.

Shared leads

Angi and HomeAdvisor sell the same homeowner to four other companies. Half never pick up.

Speed wins

Homeowners contact several flooring companies at once and book whoever answers first, often while the owner is on a job site.

Wasted measures

45 minutes out, then "my husband needs to be here before we decide." Half a day gone.

Quotes go quiet

The measure happens, the quote goes out, nobody follows up. It dies in a "let me think about it" pile.

Winter slowdown

An early-fall peak, then November to February gets thin for crews that still need work.

Ground rules

What the AI always does, and never does

Always says it's an AI

Every homeowner knows they're texting an AI assistant for your company. No pretending to be a person.

Never quotes a price

The quote comes from your estimator after the measure. The AI books the measure; it doesn't price the job.

Your ad spend is yours

Meta bills the ad spend directly to you, separate from our fee.

Only qualified measures hit the calendar

Outside the service area, no timeline, or decision makers can't be home? It doesn't take an estimator's slot.
