# Flooring VSL v1 — the booking page video

Built 2026-10-28 with the hormozi-vsl skill, from Jonathan's brief. Positioning rules: `docs/flooring-pivot.md`.
Goes above the calendar on /book (and gets texted after someone books, so they show up warm).

- Length: about 760 spoken words, roughly 5 minutes at a relaxed talking pace.
- One big idea: **you do better work, you just don't own how clients find you. We build that for you, and it answers first.**
- Every line is meant to be said out loud. Read it to yourself once; any line that doesn't sound like you on a sales call, cut or reword it.
- `[screen: …]` = what's on screen while that line plays. Not spoken.
- Never added: price, what happens if the 20 aren't hit, "one company per area", invented flooring results.
