Step 1 of 2 · Ask these questions first

5 Questions. 5 Minutes.
Before you open a single slide.

Listen carefully. Take quick notes. You'll mirror these answers back on the next slide. That's when they lean in.

Q1 · CREWS
"How many install crews do you run, and how full is their calendar the next few weeks?"
→ Confirms fit (own crews) and sizes the open capacity. Crews ready, calendar thin is the whole pitch.
Q2 · WHERE MEASURES COME FROM
"Where do your in-home measures come from right now? Referrals, Angi, HomeAdvisor, your own ads?"
→ Listen for shared leads sold to four other companies, and for ads that stopped working.
Q3 · SPEED
"When a homeowner reaches out at 8pm on a Tuesday, who answers and how fast?"
→ Homeowners contact several flooring companies at once and book whoever answers first.
Q4 · JOB VALUE + CLOSE RATE
"What's a typical install worth to you, and out of 10 measures, how many sign?"
→ Critical for the math slide. Use their numbers, never ours.
Q5 · THE REAL PAIN
"After the measure and the quote go out, what happens next? And what's the #1 thing that frustrates you about the business right now?"
→ Quotes that go quiet and wasted measure drives usually come up here. Use their exact words later in the call. Not yours, theirs.
When they finish answering Q5 → say: "Perfect. Let me show you what we install for exactly that problem." Then open the deck.
